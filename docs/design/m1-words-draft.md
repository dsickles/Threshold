## The Partner's ending (Dan, 2026-10-20: decided)

At the test moment: Maya says **"Honey…"**, Leo answers with a jubilant **"Yes!"**, and then they both laugh. The words are subtitles; the laughs are sound effects (both of them). The same for every player.

## 1. The opening beat

**Where:** the living room, the first time the player reaches its doorway view (L1). Maya is heard from the next room, as subtitles; nobody is on screen. It plays once (Story 1.6). When it ends, the boxes are gone from the back-hall doorway and the bedroom lamp is lit beyond it. Her last line is about putting a record on, which points the player to the record player and the Partner (Dan, 2026-10-22).

**The feeling:** the first night in a house that isn't home yet. Tired, giddy, a little scared, and on the edge of something bigger than the house. Just before: the cold entry. Just after: the warm lamp down the hall, the record player.

**Its jobs:** introduce Maya without showing her; show the couple's core dynamic (he fixes, she wants him to just be in it with her; narrative-design.md); give Leo the one closed-off or open line (FR7, Story 1.6); hint at "the whole thing" (a family) without saying baby; end with a practical reason the hall opens.

### "Did you lock the door?" (Dan, 2026-10-22: chosen over the alternatives)

Built on Maya's real worry about small things, which turns into the big worry. Leo's one line is either the fixer's answer or the honest one.

| # | Who | Line (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maya | Did you lock the front door? |
| 2 | Maya | Leo… are we really doing this? The house. All of it. |
| 3a | Leo, closed-off (Stoic) | We're doing it. I've got a plan. |
| 3b | Leo, open (Vulnerable) | Honestly? I'm a little scared. The good kind. |
| 4a | Maya, after 3a | Of course you do. |
| 4b | Maya, after 3b | Me too. The good kind. |
| 5 | Maya | Now please move your boxes out of the hall. I want to put a record on. |

Only lines 3 and 4 differ; the facts, the House and the next step are the same for everyone (Story 1.6). Line 1 is the small worry before the big one. Line 5 is the practical reason the boxes move and the hall opens, and it points to the record player (the bed was delivered straight to the bedroom, so it never needs finding). All lines decided (Dan, 2026-10-22).

### Alternatives

- **"All this space."** Maya: three bedrooms, what will they do with all of it? Closed-off: a joke about his stuff filling it. Open: he admits he keeps picturing a kid in the end room. Stronger setup for the spare room, but it says "kid" out loud, which the Partner then only confirms.
- **"Pizza on the floor."** The two of them eating the last slices among the boxes (the pizza box is already a House object). Warmer and funnier, but it needs the player to imagine Maya in the room with him, which is harder with no one on screen.

## 3. Maya's list

**Where:** her nightstand in the bedroom (B2), from the opening beat. Her character in one page: over-researched, practical, funny, and quietly already thinking about a baby. Decided (Dan, 2026-10-22).

> **FIRST WEEK**
> - Change the locks
> - Smoke alarms (ALL of them)
> - Find the water shut-off
> - Leo's boxes → spare room
> - Prenatal vitamins: start now? (ask the doctor)
> - Stop googling things at 2am

The vitamins line is the only hint, and it is ordinary: people trying for a baby start them before.

## 4. Maya's note on the fridge

**Where:** the fridge door (K2), after the Partner. A small "we're planning" note in Maya's hand. Ordinary, so it isn't a warning (principle 8); gone after the Loss.

**Alternative:** a list headed "NAMES (no, not yet, but…)" with two names crossed out. Funnier, but names are a bigger promise to take away.

## 5. The note on the food dish

**Where:** inside the fridge (K2), after the Loss, taped to one of the dishes family brought. Kind, practical, a little awkward: the dark stretch's one human beat.

> Lasagna. 350° for 45 min.
> We love you both. You don't have to call.
> — Mom

"Mom" is left open on purpose: it could be either of their mothers. Decided (Dan, 2026-10-22).
