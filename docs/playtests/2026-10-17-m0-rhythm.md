# M0 Check 2: rhythm timing (DAS-68)

The bar is in [../scope.md](../scope.md), "Settled questions": after one fixed adjustment for the phone's sound delay, the average tap is within about 30 ms of the click and nearly every tap would score Good.

## How it works

From the M0 menu, Rhythm timing plays a looping click. Dan taps the pad on the beat. The screen shows the average early/late, the spread, and the last taps. A delay adjustment (5 ms and 25 ms steps, up to half a beat) shifts where "on time" sits.

Two clocks can be compared:

- **Godot:** the Partner's planned method (game-architecture 4.4): playback position, plus time since the last mix, minus output latency.
- **Browser** (web only): the click is scheduled in the browser's own audio system, and each tap is timed from the touch itself against what the speaker is playing right now.

The check opens on the Godot clock; Browser is the comparison toggle.

**The two clocks don't time a tap the same way.** The Godot clock is read when Godot handles the press, a frame or more after the finger lands. The browser clock uses the touch's own time. So the Godot numbers carry some input delay that the browser numbers don't. Keep that in mind before choosing the Partner's clock from these results.

The web build plays sound even with the iPhone's silent switch on.

## Results

2026-10-17, from the itch.io build. Browser clock rows: 2026-10-18, build 1d55514.

| Setup | Fixed adjustment needed | Held steady? | Bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laptop Chrome, Godot clock | about −65 ms | Yes | Pass |
| iPhone 17 Pro Safari, Godot clock | about −150 ms | No: the average drifted while Dan tapped on the beat | **Fail** |
| iPhone 17 Pro Safari, Browser clock | about −70 ms | Mostly: the average wandered between runs (20 ms early, then 47 ms early) but did not slide one way | Run 1 pass, run 2 just misses the 30 ms average |

### iPhone Browser clock runs

Delay fixed at −70 ms for both runs, Clear taps between them. Each line is the last 20 taps.

| Run | Average | Spread | Good (±100 ms, placeholder) | 10 newest taps, newest first (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 ms early | ±38 ms | 20 of 20 | −84 −60 −43 −43 −51 −18 −52 −44 −77 −60 |
| 2 | 47 ms early | ±25 ms | 19 of 20 | −86 −52 −36 −28 −44 −19 −19 −28 −20 −20 |

In run 1 the older 10 taps averaged about 13 ms late and the newest 10 about 53 ms early. In run 2 the older 10 averaged about 59 ms early, the next few about 20 ms early, and the newest about 86 ms early. The offset moves both ways, unlike the one-way drift on the Godot clock, so some of it may be the tapper rather than the clock.
