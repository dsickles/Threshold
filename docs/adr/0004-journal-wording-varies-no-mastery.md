# The Stoic/Vulnerable choice changes journal wording; no Mastery in v1

A chapter still has one result: every player gets the same story facts, the same House objects, and the same big story beats. One thing may differ: the **closed-off (Stoic) or open (Vulnerable) answer** changes the spoken words of that scene **and the wording of the matching journal entry**. It is cheap (text only) and makes the journal feel like Leo's.

**Mastery** (optional harder goals that leave a keepsake in the House) is **not in v1**. It is listed under "Later" in [docs/scope.md](../scope.md).
