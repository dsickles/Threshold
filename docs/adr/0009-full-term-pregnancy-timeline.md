# The IVF pregnancy runs a normal full term, so the story runs about 18 months

FR17 put the IVF positive test at the end of month 9 and the Arrival at month 14. That is a pregnancy of about five months. The journal pages are dated, and the game is partly for people who have been through this, so they would notice. There is no premature birth in the story: the baby is born on a normal schedule, about 40 weeks (Dan, 2026-10-25).

The months before the IVF test stay as they were. The pregnancy chapters move to the real weeks:

| Chapter | Month | Why that month |
|---|---|---|
| Partner | 0 | Unchanged |
| Protector (the Loss) | 2 | Unchanged |
| Novice | 3–9 | Unchanged; the IVF test is at the end of month 9 |
| Provider | 10 | Unchanged; the bills land early in the pregnancy |
| Tuner | 12 | About 12 weeks: the first time a home doppler can find a heartbeat |
| Anchor | 17 | About 8 months pregnant: Maya can't lift; the crib goes up |
| Mourner | 17 | Nesting, the last weeks |
| Arrival | 18 | Term, about 40 weeks |

Play order doesn't change, and FR33's pacing still holds (the quiet Mourner follows the stressful Provider). The gap between the Tuner and the Anchor is about five months of the pregnancy that the House shows only by how it changes.
