# Build a rough emotional slice first, then the whole game rough, then depth

The first milestone is **Spark to Silence**: a rough House, a short Partner, the Loss, the Silence in the car, and the House in grief. It replaces "Epic 1 = House + Partner + menus, save, settings, journal and look system". The Partner alone only tests joy, the safest emotion. The Loss is the biggest creative risk, and it only works right after joy. After that comes **The Whole Story, Rough** (every chapter as a short rough version, in play order), then chapter depth, then polish. Menus, settings, quality and saves move to M2 or later. The order is in [docs/roadmap.md](../roadmap.md).

## Consequences

- The eight epics stay as units of chapter content. Their stories in [docs/design/stories/](../design/stories/) are reference acceptance criteria, not a build plan.
- The Anchor gets a cheap test during M2 instead of being left untested until last.
