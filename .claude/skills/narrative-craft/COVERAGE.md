# Coverage checklist

A "did we miss anything" list for questions that cover a whole chapter or the whole House. Adapted from the narrative design workflow in the BMAD Game Dev Studio module (MIT licence, BMad Code, LLC). Use it to spot gaps, not as a questionnaire for Dan.

## Story

- Premise and themes the moment serves (narrative-design.md: Transition, Vulnerability, Resilience, Connection)
- Where it sits in the story: the beat before, the beat after, the month (FR17)
- Pacing: tension rising or falling here; where the breathing room is
- The one beat of human awkwardness, if it is a dark stretch

## Characters

- What Leo wants here and what he does instead (he is a fixer who must learn to be present)
- What Maya wants (to be heard, not fixed)
- Whether either is seen, heard, or only implied
- How this moment moves Leo's arc from "Me" to "Us"

## Place

- Which rooms and viewpoints are involved, and which are open or shut
- Set dressing: what the objects say
- Light and colour (house-hope, house-grief, lamp, fog)
- Sound: the hum, rain, music, silence
- Found things: the journal, the test, papers, anything the player can pick up

## Delivery

- Story beat, chapter, or House change: which carries this
- Words needed, and where (left to Dan)
- What is required and what can be missed
- Subtitles only (no voice acting in v1, ADR-0005)

## Gameplay integration

- Does the mechanic say the same thing as the story? (Ludonarrative harmony: a game about being present shouldn't reward rushing.)
- Story gate: what opens, what closes, and why
- Agency: tone (Stoic/Vulnerable) only, never outcome

## Production

- Build cost in the current milestone
- New art or sound needed (free asset packs first, AI only to fill gaps, ADR-0010)
- Words added against the budget (about 5,000 dialogue, 2,000 journal, NFR8)
- Tests and success criteria from the roadmap and stories that apply

## Consistency

- No conflict with the GDD, scope.md or any ADR (if there is one, say so; don't pick silently)
- Every touched ledger entry still has one clean lifeline
