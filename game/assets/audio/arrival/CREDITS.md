# The Arrival's sounds

Every file here is a **PLACEHOLDER**, generated by
`tools/arrival/make_placeholder_audio.py` (no recordings, no outside sources),
for the rough M2 version (DAS-137). Real sounds are picked later under
ADR-0010; the look and feel wait on Dan's photos (story-beats.md §8).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `placeholder_zip.wav`, `placeholder_pop.wav` | The bag zipped shut, and bursting open on the first try |
| `placeholder_keys.wav`, `placeholder_door.wav` | The car keys picked up, the front door opening |
| `placeholder_static.wav`, `placeholder_cry.wav` | The cry beat: static, and a newborn's cry (loops) |
| `placeholder_heart_song.mp3` | The hand beat's track: a baby's heartbeat, 120 a minute, with a soft chord on every other beat (the notes to tap) |
| `placeholder_breath.wav` | A baby breathing, for the quiet hold at the end (loop) |
